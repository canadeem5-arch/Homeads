THE REAL PROBLEM

More Leads Don't Always Mean More Sales

Real estate businesses can generate hundreds of enquiries and still struggle to create consistent sales opportunities. Poor targeting, generic landing pages, weak qualification, slow follow-up and disconnected lead systems can reduce the value of otherwise useful traffic.

01. Unqualified Enquiries

Sales teams spend time responding to people who may not match the property's budget, location or requirements.

02. Generic Landing Pages

Sending every campaign visitor to the same homepage can create unnecessary friction between interest and enquiry.

03. Poor Lead Qualification

Without useful qualification questions, sales teams may receive limited information about buyer intent.

04. Disconnected Lead Sources

Leads from websites, advertising platforms and messaging channels can become difficult to manage when systems are not connected.

05. Slow Follow-Up

A valuable property enquiry can lose momentum when the sales team does not receive or act on it quickly.

06. No Clear Attribution

Without appropriate tracking, it can become difficult to understand which campaigns, pages and channels generate meaningful enquiries.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Real Estate Lead Generation Questions

What is real estate lead generation? +

Real estate lead generation is the process of attracting potential property buyers, investors, tenants or other prospects and converting their interest into enquiries that a sales team can follow up.

Can HomeAds generate leads for individual property projects? +

Yes. Lead-generation experiences can be designed around individual projects, properties, developments, locations or campaigns.

Do you create landing pages for lead-generation campaigns? +

Yes. HomeAds can create dedicated property and project landing pages designed around the campaign objective and target audience.

Can you connect leads to our CRM? +

Where the CRM provides suitable integration methods such as APIs, webhooks or supported connectors, website enquiries can be connected according to the agreed workflow.

Can you integrate WhatsApp? +

Yes. WhatsApp can be incorporated into property websites and landing pages as a direct communication and enquiry channel, with deeper automation depending on the technical setup.

Can you qualify property leads? +

Yes. Landing pages and enquiry forms can collect relevant information such as property interest, budget, location, property type and purchase timeline.

Do you guarantee a specific number of leads? +

No. Lead volume and quality depend on factors including property demand, market conditions, targeting, campaign budget, offer, landing page experience and sales follow-up. HomeAds focuses on building and optimizing the digital lead-generation system.

Do you provide mobile app development? +

No. HomeAds focuses on real estate web development, website design, WordPress websites, landing pages and web-based lead-generation systems. Mobile application development is not part of this service.
