FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Real Estate CRM Questions

How can a CRM benefit my real estate business? +

A CRM can help organize property enquiries, customer information, follow-ups and sales opportunities in one structured system. When connected to your website, it can reduce disconnected lead handling and give your team better visibility.

Can HomeAds connect my WordPress website to my existing CRM? +

Yes, where the CRM provides suitable integration methods such as APIs, webhooks or supported connectors. The exact integration depends on your CRM platform, account permissions and technical requirements.

Can you create property-specific lead forms? +

Yes. Forms can be structured around individual properties, developments, locations, campaigns or enquiry types.

Can website enquiries automatically reach our CRM? +

Where the required CRM integration and API access are available, website enquiries can be connected to the CRM according to the agreed workflow.

Can you connect WhatsApp with the website? +

Yes, HomeAds can design WhatsApp enquiry experiences and, where technically supported, connect communication workflows with relevant systems.

Can our team manage the website through WordPress? +

Yes. WordPress allows authorized team members to manage website content, pages and configured dynamic content through the administration system.

Do you provide mobile app development? +

No. HomeAds focuses on web design, WordPress websites, web development and website-based CRM integrations. We do not provide mobile application development.

Can the CRM system be customized for our sales process? +

The website and integration workflow can be structured around your requirements. The level of CRM customization depends on the CRM platform and project scope.
