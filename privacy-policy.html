Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

At HomeAds Real Estate Digital Solutions, we are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of property developers, brokers, and high-net-worth real estate investors worldwide and worldwide.

01. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how HomeAds Real Estate Digital Solutions ("HomeAds", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, protects, and discloses information obtained from visitors, clients, and prospective investors interacting with our web markets, landing pages, digital consultation channels, and lead generation infrastructure.

We comply fully with the world Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 regarding Personal Data Protection (PDPL) and ensure strict alignment with Land Department (DLD) and Trakheesi marketing compliance frameworks.

02. Information We Collect

In delivering bespoke real estate web design, property market engineering, and high-intent lead generation services, we may collect the following categories of data:

  • Contact Information: Full name, phone number, WhatsApp contact identifier, email address, company name, and job title.
  • Property Inquiries & Preferences: Budget range, target investment zones (e.g., Palm Jumeirah, Downtown ), off-plan unit preferences, and consultation requests.
  • Technical Analytics Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, geolocation data, session duration, and referral source URLs.
  • Form Submissions: Information submitted via consultation request forms, brochure downloads, and live chat widgets.

03. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate business and service delivery purposes, including:

  • Scheduling private web design and digital strategy consultations.
  • Routing verified investor inquiries directly to client real estate CRMs and sales teams.
  • Customizing interactive floor plan displays, 3D walkthroughs, and off-plan markets based on investor interactions.
  • Improving website speed, mobile responsiveness, and conversion performance.
  • Fulfilling legal, regulatory, and DLD Trakheesi compliance obligations.

🔒 Zero Lead Resale Commitment: We never sell, rent, or trade your investor leads or confidential real estate client data to third-party brokers or external data aggregators.

04. Data Security & Storage

We employ enterprise-grade technical and organizational security measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of personal data:

  • End-to-end SSL encryption for all web data transmissions and form posts.
  • Secure API integrations connecting web forms directly to Google Workspace and real estate CRMs.
  • Restricted access control policies granting access only to authorized HomeAds technical staff.
  • Regular vulnerability monitoring and system security updates.

05. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes essential cookies and session analytics to optimize site performance and measure campaign conversion rates. You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings without affecting core browsing capability.

06. Your Rights & Choice

Under applicable data protection regulations, you retain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to the personal data we store about you.
  • Requesting correction or updates to inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of your contact records from our CRM database.
  • Opting out of promotional emails or WhatsApp communications at any time.
